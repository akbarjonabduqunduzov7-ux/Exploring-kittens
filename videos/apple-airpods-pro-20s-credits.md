# Apple — The world can wait

Independent 20-second AirPods Pro concept, not an Apple advertisement or endorsement. Native HyperFrames 0.9.9 composition; English typography; no voiceover.

Music: **Drive — Zane Little**, CC0 1.0 Universal. [Artist/track license listing](https://freemusicarchive.org/music/zane-little/new-peach-radio/drive-3/). MP3 mirrored by [DJ Drosophila](https://github.com/MRsagi/dj-drosophila/blob/main/LICENSES.md). Excerpt, normalization and volume-envelope edits documented in assets/ledger.json. The track was not generated for this project.

Product imagery: original transparent Apple-site artwork mirrored in [this community reference project](https://github.com/dannykeqiang-sys/airpods-pro-clone). Existing Apple logo vector paths mirrored in [this reference project](https://github.com/humblotj/airpods-pro). No product images or UI screens were invented. No latest-model, inventory, pricing or numerical performance claim.

Noise-cancellation terminology checked against [Apple Support](https://support.apple.com/en-gb/108918). “The world can wait” is original concept copy, not an official tagline or a claim that outside sound is completely eliminated.

Motion typography: Manrope, OFL-1.1. SFX: seeded Earshot 0.4.0 procedural bank, MIT. Animation: GSAP through the native HyperFrames timeline.
